Every order ships from our shop in Southern California.

Processing time

Orders go out within 2–5 business days. We're a small operation — your shirt is packed by the people who made the brand, not a warehouse robot. You'll get a confirmation email when your order is placed and a tracking number the moment it ships.

Rates & delivery

Shipping rates are calculated at checkout. Orders ship via USPS with tracking. Typical delivery within the US is 3–7 business days after your order ships.

We currently ship within the United States only. Want something shipped elsewhere? Email hello@yardmicrowaves.com and we'll see what we can do.

Carrier delays

Once a package is with the carrier, transit times are their department — we can't control weather, holidays, or USPS having a moment. If your tracking hasn't moved in more than 5 business days, email us and we'll chase it.

Lost, damaged, or wrong orders

If your order arrives damaged, is misprinted, or the wrong item shows up, email hello@yardmicrowaves.com within 7 days of delivery with a photo and your order number — we'll make it right per our Refund Policy.

If tracking says delivered but there's no package, give it 48 hours (carriers sometimes scan early), check with neighbors, then email us.

Address problems

Double-check your address at checkout — we ship to exactly what you enter. If a package comes back to us as undeliverable, we'll reach out; reshipping is at your cost. Caught a typo right after ordering? Email us ASAP and we'll fix it if the order hasn't shipped.
