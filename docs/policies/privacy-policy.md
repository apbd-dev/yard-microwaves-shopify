# Privacy Policy

*Effective July 29, 2026 · Published at `/policies/privacy-policy` (Shopify Admin → Settings → Policies)*

Yard Microwaves ("we", "us") runs this store. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit or buy from yardmicrowaves.com, and what we do with it. Short version: we collect what's needed to sell you a shirt, and we don't sell your data to anyone.

## What we collect

**Order information.** When you buy something we collect your name, email, shipping address, and payment details. Payments are processed by our payment providers — we never see or store your full card number.

**Contact information.** If you sign up for our email list or email us, we keep your email address and the conversation.

**Device information.** Like most sites, we automatically collect some data when you browse — IP address, browser, pages viewed — via cookies and similar tech. Our store runs on Shopify, which uses this to keep the site working and give us basic analytics (see [Shopify's privacy policy](https://www.shopify.com/legal/privacy)).

## How we use it

- **Fulfill orders** — charge you, ship to you, send order and shipping confirmations, handle returns.
- **Support** — answer your emails.
- **Marketing** — send you emails about drops and new stuff, **only if you opted in**. Every email has an unsubscribe link that works.
- **Keep the store running** — screen for fraud, fix problems, understand what people look at.

## Who we share it with

Only the services that make the store work: **Shopify** (our store platform), our **payment processors**, **shipping carriers** (they need your address to deliver), and our **email platform** for the mailing list. Each gets only what it needs to do its job. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

**We do not sell or rent your personal information. Ever.**

## Cookies

Shopify sets cookies needed for the cart and checkout to function, plus analytics cookies. You can control cookies through your browser settings; blocking the functional ones will break checkout.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (including California under the CCPA and the EU/UK under GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, and to know how it's used. We honor these requests regardless of where you live: email **hello@yardmicrowaves.com** and we'll take care of it. We'll never treat you differently for exercising your rights.

## Data retention

We keep order records as long as we need them for taxes, accounting, and handling returns. Mailing-list data is kept until you unsubscribe.

## Kids

This store isn't directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their information.

## Changes

If we change this policy, the new version goes up on this page with a new effective date.

## Contact

Questions about your data: **hello@yardmicrowaves.com**.
