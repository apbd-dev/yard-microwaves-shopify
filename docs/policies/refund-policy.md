# Refund Policy

*Effective July 29, 2026 · Published at `/policies/refund-policy` (Shopify Admin → Settings → Policies)*

We want you to actually wear this stuff. If something's not right, we'll fix it.

## Returns

You have **30 days** from the day your order arrives to request a return. To be eligible, apparel must be **unworn, unwashed, and in its original condition** with any tags attached.

**Stickers and sticker packs are final sale** — they're stickers.

To start a return, email **hello@yardmicrowaves.com** with your order number and what's going on. We'll send you the return address and instructions. Items sent back without requesting a return first won't be accepted.

Return shipping is on you, unless we messed up (see below).

## Damaged, defective, or wrong items

If your order shows up damaged, misprinted, or just plain wrong, email us within **7 days** of delivery with a photo and your order number. We'll replace it or refund you in full — including shipping — no need to send anything back unless we ask.

## Exchanges

Wrong size? Email us within 30 days and we'll swap it, subject to stock. Fastest route: request a return and place a new order for the size you want so it doesn't sell out while your return is in transit.

## Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your return, we'll notify you and issue the refund to your **original payment method within 10 business days**. Your bank or card issuer may take a few extra days to post it. If more than 15 business days have passed since we approved your refund, email **hello@yardmicrowaves.com**.

Original shipping costs are non-refundable, except when the return is our fault.
